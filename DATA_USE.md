# Data and output terms

The MIT License in `LICENSE` applies to the software in `code/`, the workflow
configuration in `.github/`, and the repository's command-line support files.
It does not relicense scientific observations or source-derived data.

## Source data

Files in `data/benchmark/` reproduce data tables released by their original
sources. Their reuse remains subject to the terms of those sources. Users are
responsible for checking the applicable terms and citing the original articles
and data records.

## Registries and archived panels

Files in `data/registries/` and `data/external_panels/` contain project-created
provenance annotations, screening decisions, harmonized identifiers, or direct
transcriptions from cited source records. They are supplied to make the
analysis auditable. This repository does not claim ownership of the underlying
scientific observations, and their inclusion does not override source terms.
Article and repository identifiers are retained where available so users can
verify and cite the original records.

## Derived results

Files in `results/` are computational outputs generated from the included data
and code. They may be regenerated for verification, but their distribution
does not expand the reuse rights attached to the underlying source data.

No article full text, downloaded literature PDF, or publisher-owned figure is
distributed in this repository.
